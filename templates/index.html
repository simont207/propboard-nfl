How the numbers on this site work

Hit rate — the % of a player's recent games where the actual stat cleared the line shown (e.g. 8/10 means he went over in 8 of his last 10 games).

Matchup rank — how the opponent's defense ranks (1st to 32nd/last) against that position. 1st means the defense allows the most to that position — the best matchup for the Over.

Zone Edge (NFL receiving props only) — the % of a player's own targets that land in areas of the field where this week's specific opponent has allowed the highest catch rate.

All of these describe recent results, not predictions — see the note below.

Sportsbook lines

Right now the lines in the table are estimates from recent averages. To see real FanDuel / DraftKings prop lines, add a free key from The Odds API:

  1. Go to the-odds-api.com and sign up for the free plan.
  2. Copy the key they email you and paste it below.
  3. Click Save key, then Pull lines now.

A pull uses about 7 credits per game (~110 for a full slate). The free plan gives 500 a month, so pull once or twice a week. Nothing is pulled automatically.

SportsGameOdds (NFL, richer data)

A second, separate real-lines source with 9 books, real alt-line prices, and a fair (no-vig) line to check each book's price against — that's what powers the EV numbers you'll see once you pull. NFL only, and it's its own key (saved to data/sgo_key.txt, not entered here).

One pull costs about 16-20 credits (one per game shown) — 2,500 free a month, so this comfortably supports pulling every day if you want. Also never runs automatically.
