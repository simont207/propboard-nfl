Sportsbook lines

Right now the lines in the table are estimates from recent averages. To see real FanDuel / DraftKings prop lines, add a free key from The Odds API:

  1. Go to the-odds-api.com and sign up for the free plan.
  2. Copy the key they email you and paste it below.
  3. Click Save key, then Pull lines now.

A pull uses about 7 credits per game (~110 for a full slate). The free plan gives 500 a month, so pull once or twice a week. Nothing is pulled automatically.
